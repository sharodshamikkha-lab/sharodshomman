About us — আমরা কারা

We are Team Sharod Shammikhha 2026.

A new, independent celebration where Durga Puja meets art, culture and creativity.

We believe a Puja is more than a pandal. It is an expression of people's ideas, emotions, beliefs and imagination. Our jury and team look beyond simply judging a pandal. We experience the story behind it, the artistry within it, and the spirit that brings it to life.

From Sabekiana to Aitihya, from creativity to modernism, we celebrate the beautiful contrast between tradition and tomorrow.

This Sharod, come experience Durga Puja with us, share your creativity, and become a part of something new.

Join us. Celebrate with us.
Be the Shreshto Sharod Shammikhha 2026.
