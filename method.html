Method — পদ্ধতি

Serious, transparent, repeatable.

The same notebook page for every puja. Pencil first, ink later. If we couldn't check something, we leave it blank — we don't guess. Final scores: jury 70% + verified public notes 30%.

01

Observe

Field visits from Dwitiya to Panchami. Day + night. Photos, measurements, artisan interviews, sound readings after 10pm.

02

Document

Standard dossier per puja: theme note, materials, named artists, lighting plan, accessibility audit, queue behaviour.

03

Evaluate

Independent jury scoring on eight dimensions. Public notes verified and weighted at 30%. Dissent is printed, not hidden.

04

Honour

Results declared on Soshthi. Eight honours + People's Choice. Scoresheets, jury notes and counts published.

The rubric — 150 points

DimensionWhat we checkPoints
CreativityOriginality of concept, material risk, freshness. A small twist counts.25
Artistic excellenceComposition, detailing, harmony. We walk behind the pandal too.25
Idol CraftsmanshipAnatomy, expression, finish — kathamo to chokkhu-daan. Maker named.20
Theme & storytellingCan a 12-year-old get it in 5 minutes? Does it stay after Dashami?20
LightingNight visit only. Glare, queue comfort, resident sleep. Brightest ≠ best.15
SustainabilityMaterials, waste, sound, river footprint. Verified at the ghat on Ekadashi.15
AccessibilityRamps, senior queues, water, toilets, safety. Field-tested.15
TraditionRitual precision — dhak, arati timing, bhog, family continuity.15
Jury score70%

Four jurors score independently. Highest and lowest are discussed, not averaged blindly.

Public notes30%

Verified visits only — day + time required. "Nice pandal" doesn't count; specifics do.

Published100%

Scoresheets, counts, dissent and corrections. Mistakes are crossed, not erased.
